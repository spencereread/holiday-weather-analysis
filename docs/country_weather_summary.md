
## Bosnia & Herzegovina
### 3–10 April (1991–2025)

**Typical afternoon maximum:**  
14–15°C

**Typical overnight minimum:**  
5–7°C

**Chance of reaching 20°C:**  
2–13%

**Chance of a wet day (>1 mm):**  
44–46%

**Summary:**  
Bosnia shows the greatest variability with both warm days and cool nights possible.
